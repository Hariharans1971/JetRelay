# JetRelay global pilot launch

31 October 2026. This is a launch plan and prepared campaign copy, not a claim that campaigns ran or human tests took place.

## Current product position

JetRelay is a public pilot for global aviation references and operator-controlled whole-aircraft empty-leg workflows. The active database is on Neon Free, which currently has no fixed trial expiry. Website hosting remains Render Free with idle sleep. Quotas and provider terms apply. Payment/email activation and verified production inventory remain outstanding. Public access does not establish permission to sell flights in every jurisdiction.

Public invitation: https://aeroshare-global.onrender.com/pilot.html

## Competitor research and product decisions

Victor's public FAQ describes operator names, aircraft tail numbers, actual aircraft images and cancellation conditions. It also warns that empty-leg timing and availability can change at short notice. JetRelay should make these disclosures understandable before a hold or payment, and only display independently verified inventory.

LunaJets describes empty legs as repositioning opportunities and stresses flexibility. Its public website supports several languages and regional contact points. JetRelay should validate demand and operational support by region before adding translated marketing or promising round-the-clock assistance.

These are useful market benchmarks. No claim is made that JetRelay is unique, superior or affiliated with these brands.

## First participants

Recruit a small voluntary cohort before broad advertising: five prospective travellers, three aviation professionals or charter operators, and two people using different mobile devices. Aim for participants in India, the UAE, Europe, North America and Southeast Asia where feasible. These are recruitment targets, not completed tests.

Use the public five-minute test page. Participants can submit successful or failed tasks through the GitHub pilot-feedback form. Country/region is optional, and reports are public. Collect no identity documents, card details, passwords or private customer bookings. Account and inventory tests need owner-coordinated staging accounts; do not seed fake inventory into production.

Record each test in a private owner-controlled research log: participant consent, broad region, device/browser, task, result, issue reference and resolution. Mark unknown values as unknown. Publish only aggregated findings with permission. No participant has yet been recruited or tested by this work.

## Rollout sequence

| Phase | Audience and channel | Evidence before expanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public usability pilot | Owner's professional social accounts and opted-in aviation/product communities | At least 10 genuine participant reports and no unresolved account-isolation or data-loss defect |
| Operator discovery | Introductions through the owner's actual industry contacts and permission-based operator groups | Independently verified operators and documented operational feedback |
| Regional controlled pilot | One supported operating market at a time | Merchant/email acceptance tests, approved real inventory, contracts, refunds, support and recovery process |
| Broader acquisition | Search content, partnerships and regional campaigns | Reliable conversion, support capacity, provider quotas and an approved acquisition budget |

Start with the empty-leg explanation and product testing, rather than advertising flights that are not available. Do not buy contact lists, send bulk unsolicited messages, impersonate an airline, post fake reviews, or imply official approval.

## Measurement and boundaries

Track real submitted reports, unique consenting participants, task completion, unresolved defects, approved operator applications and actual support requests. Campaign impressions, visitors, bookings and regional reach remain unmeasured until an appropriate analytics/social account is connected and data collection is configured. Avoid promising universal aircraft visibility, guaranteed discounts, lifetime free hosting or uninterrupted availability.
