Help shape JetRelay

Aviation professionals and curious travellers can help us build a useful, clear private aviation platform.

This is a product pilot. Payment collection and email activation are pending. Only approved operator inventory can appear as a flight. No discount or worldwide radar coverage is guaranteed.

A five minute test

  1. Open the airport directory. Search an airport you know, such as Mumbai, Dubai, London or New York. Check the codes, map location and source date.
  2. Open flight radar. Look at the observation time and source. If the feed is unavailable, confirm that the page explains it clearly.
  3. Try the empty-leg search. Empty results are expected until operators submit and approve real flights. Check whether the total-price and cancellation explanations are understandable.
  4. Open the client, operator and administrator access pages. Confirm that each identifies its role. Do not attempt to access another person's account.
  5. Check service availability and read the guide. Tell us which instruction or error message needs improvement.

Share what happened

Record your browser, device type, country or broad region, the page you tested, and what you expected. Include the steps needed to reproduce an issue. You can also tell us that a task worked successfully.

Feedback is submitted through GitHub and is public. A GitHub account is required. Never include passwords, authentication codes, API keys, private booking details, identity documents or payment information. Remove personal details from screenshots.
