JetRelay

Pilot terms and privacy notice

JetRelay is a software marketplace pilot. The platform does not operate aircraft, provide flight clearance or certify an operator’s legal authority. Only an authorised aircraft operator can confirm carriage. This notice does not replace a reviewed broker agreement, carrier contract, refund policy or jurisdiction-specific privacy policy.

Listings and bookings

Empty legs depend on a parent charter and can change or disappear when that charter changes. Prices are for the whole aircraft in the displayed currency. Any taxes, charges and exclusions must be disclosed in the operator’s approved offer and final contract. A hold is temporary and is not a ticket. Payment success requires server verification. Travel requires flight-specific operator confirmation.

Account and private data

The account system stores your name, email, password hash, role and security-session records in PostgreSQL once the owner connects it. Booking records include selected routes, passenger count, timestamps, prices and status. Documents use application-layer encryption and authenticated downloads. Saved aircraft preferences remain in browser storage. JetRelay does not collect card numbers; configured checkout runs on the payment provider’s domain.

Pilot data limits

Use test information during pilot evaluation. Do not upload passports, government identifiers, medical records or payment credentials. Retention, deletion, incident response and support contact details must be finalised by the owner before commercial customer onboarding. No public claim of regulatory approval or security certification is made.

Reference information

Airport, map, weather and aircraft observation data are informational. Source snapshots and provider coverage have limitations. They must not be used for dispatch, navigation, flight-safety or availability decisions.

Commercial activation

Live collection remains blocked until merchant configuration and the owner’s commercial launch review are complete. The owner must provide reviewed contracts, refund terms, a legal identity and support channels before selling travel.
